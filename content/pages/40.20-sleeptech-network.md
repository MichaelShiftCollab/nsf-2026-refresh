# **SleepTech® Network**

Calling all sleep technology innovators\! Whether you’re a growing start-up or a global brand, the National Sleep Foundation’s SleepTech® Network gives you a front-row seat to the future of sleep health. Connect with industry leaders, stay on top of cutting-edge science and be part of shaping what’s next in sleep technology.  
The NSF SleepTech® Network comprises industry leaders dedicated to advancing the promise of technology for sleep health. NSF is committed to providing expert content and data-driven resources to help innovators best serve consumer needs and public health. NSF provides evidence-based and consensus-driven recommendations and content that can help today’s innovators engage with consumers and professionals who apply technology to sleep health.

## **SleepTech® Network FAQs**

## **What are the membership levels in the SleepTech Network?**

There are three membership levels in the SleepTech Network. Basic (\$5,000/year) is the entry point for Network access for start-ups and smaller companies. Standard (\$12,500/year) includes upgraded visibility in NSF’s booth at CES. Premium (\$25,000/year) includes campaign participation and visibility featuring leadership in sleep health.

## **What are the benefits of joining the SleepTech Network?**

By joining the network, companies receive evidence-based and consensus-driven recommendations, standards, and content from NSF that can help today’s technology designers, developers, and manufacturers who are working to help improve people’s sleep. Learn more in the [SleepTech® Network Prospectus](https://www.thensf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/SleepTech_Network_2025_Prospectus.pdf).

## **What companies can become a member of the NSF SleepTech Network?**

Any company that designs, develops, manufactures, distributes, or conducts research on sleep health technology solutions can be considered for membership.

## **What is the membership year?**

Membership is on a rolling basis. Membership includes 12 months from when the company joins.

## **Can I use the National Sleep Foundation logo?**

SleepTech Network members are only allowed to use the NSF SleepTech Network logo. Use of the NSF enterprise name and mark is prohibited unless there is a separate licensing agreement in place.

## **Does my company need to be a member of the SleepTech Network to apply for the SleepTech® Award?**

No, membership in the SleepTech Network is not a prerequisite for submitting an application for the [SleepTech Award](https://www.thensf.org/sleeptech-award/).

## **Is my company eligible for membership after being selected as a SleepTech Award winner or finalist?**

Yes. Your company is eligible for SleepTech Network membership regardless of your SleepTech Award status.
