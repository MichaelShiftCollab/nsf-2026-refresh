# **NSF / CTA / ANSI Standards**

Sleep technology has incredible potential to improve consumers’ sleep and their health.  Sleep technology is making incredible progress in democratizing personal sleep health information.   
NSF and the Consumer Technology Association® (CTA), an American National Standards Institute (ANSI) accredited standards developer, partner to develop technology performance and measurement standards that inform the development of consumer sleep technology.

## **Current Standards**

[Definitions and Characteristics for Wearable Sleep Monitors](https://shop.cta.tech/products/cta-2052-1-a)  
[Methods of Measurements for Sleep Tracking Consumer Technology Solutions](https://www.cta.tech/standards/ansictansf-20522-a/)  
[Performance Criteria for Sleep Tracking Consumer Technology Solutions](https://www.cta.tech/standards/ctansf-20523-a/)  
[Performance Requirements for Sleep Solutions Detecting Snoring](https://www.cta.tech/standards/ansictansf-2092/)  
[Best Practices of Sleep Quality in Consumer Sleep Solutions](https://www.cta.tech/standards/ansictansf-2110/)
