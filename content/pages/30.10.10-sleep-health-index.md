# **Sleep Health Index®**

The *Sleep Health Index*® is the foremost index for large or small population groups to understand sleep health.  The quarterly index has over 22,000 responses over 22 consecutive periods. The *Sleep Health Index* is used globally. 

If you are interested in licensing the *Sleep Health Index*, contact the NSF at [research@thensf.org](mailto:research@thensf.org).
